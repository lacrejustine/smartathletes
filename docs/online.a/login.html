Deployment checklist

This sign-in protects the admin pages from casual access only. The credentials live in config.js and the "session" is a flag in this browser's local storage, so anyone with access to the files or developer tools can get past it.

  • Server-side authentication — validate the login on a backend over HTTPS so the browser never receives or stores the password.
  • Hashed passwords — store bcrypt, Argon2, or scrypt hashes on the server; never ship a plain-text password to the client.
  • Real sessions — httpOnly + Secure + SameSite cookies or short-lived signed tokens, with rate limiting and lockout on repeated failures.
  • Per-user accounts — separate logins and an audit trail for each staff member who touches the season.
